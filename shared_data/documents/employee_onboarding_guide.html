Before the First Day

The hiring manager confirms the employee's department, role, work location, and start date with People Operations. People Operations creates the employee's internal account and assigns the required orientation sessions. The manager prepares role-specific tasks and identifies a teammate who can answer day-to-day questions.

First Day

The employee attends the company introduction and security orientation. The manager reviews the team's purpose, communication channels, and the employee's initial priorities. The employee should contact People Operations if an account, accessibility, or onboarding item is missing.

First Week

The manager schedules a role and department overview, confirms access to approved systems, and agrees on a short list of first-week goals. The assigned teammate introduces the employee to relevant workflows and documentation.

Responsibilities

  • People Operations: coordinate standard orientation and maintain the onboarding checklist.
  • Manager: provide role context, team introductions, initial goals, and approved access requests.
  • Employee: attend scheduled orientation and raise missing setup needs through the designated company channel.

Example Questions

What should the manager prepare before the first day? Who handles standard orientation? What should an employee do if account access is missing?
