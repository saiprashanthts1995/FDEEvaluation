Leave Request Guide

Document ID: HR-GUIDE-002  |  Version: 1.0  |  Fictional training material

Demonstration document: This synthetic guide is not a real employer policy or legal advice. Actual leave eligibility and requirements depend on applicable law and employer policy.

Planned Leave

For planned time away, an employee submits a request through the company's designated leave system as early as practical and includes the dates and a work handoff plan. The employee should notify their manager that a request was submitted. The manager reviews coverage and responds through the system; a request is not considered approved until its status is marked approved there.

Unexpected Absence

If an employee cannot work as scheduled, they notify their manager as soon as practical using the normal absence-reporting channel. The manager records the notification according to company procedure and directs policy or eligibility questions to People Operations. Employees should share only the information required by the process.

Changes and Questions

Employees update or cancel a request in the leave system when plans change. Questions about eligibility, required documentation, or an unresolved request should go to People Operations. Managers should not make eligibility determinations outside the documented process.

Example Questions

Where is a planned leave request submitted? When is a request approved? Who should answer eligibility questions? What should an employee do about an unexpected absence?
